Support

Contact Vybz

Last updated: 14 May 2026

Use this page for Vybz support, safety, privacy, moderation, account access, and account deletion help.

Support email

vybzsocialapp@gmail.com

What to include

To help us respond, include your Vybz username, the email linked to your account, a short explanation of the issue, and any relevant screenshots. Do not send passwords or private login codes.

Account deletion

You can request account deletion inside Vybz from Settings where available. If you cannot access your account, email support and include the email address connected to your Vybz account.

Safety and moderation

For urgent safety or moderation concerns, report content in the app where available and email support with the username, post details, and reason for the report.

Response times

Vybz is currently in testing and response times may vary. We will prioritise safety, privacy, and account access issues.
