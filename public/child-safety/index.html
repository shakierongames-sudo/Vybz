Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 14 May 2026

Vybz has zero tolerance for content or behaviour that exploits, harms, endangers, sexualises, targets, or abuses minors. These standards apply to all users, posts, profiles, images, captions, reports, and interactions on Vybz.

1. Prohibited content and conduct

Vybz does not allow child sexual abuse material, child sexual exploitation, grooming, sexualised content involving minors, attempts to contact minors for unsafe purposes, trafficking, coercion, threats, or any content that places a minor at risk.

2. Reporting

Users can report unsafe content or accounts in the app where reporting tools are available. Concerns can also be sent to vybzsocialapp@gmail.com. Reports involving child safety are prioritised.

3. Review and enforcement

Vybz may remove content, restrict accounts, suspend accounts, ban accounts, preserve relevant evidence where appropriate, and take other action needed to protect users and comply with applicable law.

4. Escalation

Where required, Vybz will report apparent child safety violations to the relevant authorities or legally designated reporting channels in the applicable region.

5. Contact point

The designated Vybz contact for child safety standards, reports, and compliance is vybzsocialapp@gmail.com.
