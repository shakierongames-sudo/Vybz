Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vybz handles information when you use the Vybz app, website, Android app, and related testing builds. Vybz is a social/photo-rating app where users can create profiles, upload posts, rate content, follow other users, and receive activity notifications.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Vybz, we may collect or store:

  • Account information, such as your email address, authentication provider, user ID, and age-gate confirmation metadata.
  • Profile information, such as username, display name, bio, avatar, vibe colour, and profile preferences.
  • User content, such as uploaded images, captions, categories, moods, and post settings.
  • Interaction data, such as ratings, follows, blocks, reports, activity notifications, and account deletion requests.
  • Moderation data, such as reports, account restrictions, post removals, and admin actions.
  • Technical data, such as basic device, browser, hosting, and app delivery information needed to run and secure the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage your Vybz account;
  • show profiles, posts, ratings, follows, and activity features;
  • store and display uploaded images and profile avatars;
  • protect the app through reporting, moderation, blocking, and admin tools;
  • handle account deletion requests and support queries;
  • improve stability, safety, and user experience during testing and future releases.

3. Public content

Vybz is a social app. Content you post publicly, including images, captions, profile names, usernames, ratings, and public profile details, may be visible to other users of the app. Do not upload private, sensitive, illegal, or harmful content.

4. Service providers

Vybz uses third-party services to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication, database, and storage, Netlify for hosting and deployment, and Google Play for Android app distribution and testing. These providers may process data as needed to provide their services.

5. Login and authentication

Vybz supports email sign-in and Google sign-in through Supabase Auth. Authentication data is used to identify your account and keep your session active. We do not ask for your Google password.

6. Age requirement

Vybz is intended for users aged 13 or over. Users must confirm they meet the age requirement before creating or using an account. If we learn that an account does not meet the age requirement, we may restrict or remove it.

7. Account deletion

You can request account deletion from the app settings. During the beta/testing period, account deletion requests may be reviewed and handled manually by an admin. You can also contact support at vybzsocialapp@gmail.com for deletion help.

8. Data retention

We keep account, profile, post, moderation, and interaction data for as long as needed to operate Vybz, protect users, comply with platform requirements, and handle support or deletion requests. Deleted or removed content may remain in backups or logs for a limited period where technically necessary.

9. Safety and moderation

Vybz includes reporting, blocking, post removal, account restriction, suspension, and ban tools. We may review content and account activity to enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines.

10. Changes to this policy

Vybz is still developing, so this policy may be updated as the app changes. The latest version will be available on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy, safety, support, or account deletion questions, contact: vybzsocialapp@gmail.com.
