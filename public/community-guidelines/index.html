Safety

Community Guidelines

Last updated: 14 May 2026

Vybz is for sharing everyday moments, outfits, food, fitness, gaming, travel, music, study, work, pets, art, random vibes, and other safe content. These guidelines explain what is and is not allowed.

1. Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, threats, intimidation, targeted abuse, or attempts to shame other users. Ratings should be used as a light interaction feature, not as a way to attack people.

2. Keep content safe

Do not post illegal, dangerous, exploitative, hateful, graphically violent, sexually explicit, or harmful content. Do not encourage self-harm, violence, abuse, criminal activity, or unsafe behaviour.

3. Protect privacy

Do not share private information, addresses, phone numbers, private messages, financial details, login details, or images of people who have not agreed to be posted.

4. No hate or discrimination

Do not attack, dehumanise, or exclude people based on protected characteristics such as race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, or age.

5. Young people and safety

Vybz is for users aged 13 or over. Do not post content that exploits, endangers, sexualises, targets, or exposes minors to unsafe situations.

6. Authentic use

Do not impersonate people, spam the app, manipulate ratings, create accounts to evade bans, or use Vybz to scam or mislead others.

7. Reporting and blocking

If something feels unsafe, report it in the app where available or contact support. You can also block users when needed. Admins may review reports and take action.

8. Moderation actions

Vybz may remove posts, restrict accounts, suspend accounts, ban accounts, or take other steps to keep the app safe and usable.

9. Contact

For safety, moderation, privacy, or account deletion help, email vybzsocialapp@gmail.com.
